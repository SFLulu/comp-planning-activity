The two-variable habit

Most managers allocate on performance alone. That quietly widens pay gaps within the same role, year after year, without anyone deciding it should happen.

A strong performer already high in range needs less than an equally strong performer sitting low in range.

Rating tells you what someone earned. Pay position tells you where the money should go.
