Back on your own team

Before your next cycle, pull two numbers for every person on your team.

You’ll already have the first one. The second is the one managers skip — and it’s the one that changes the answer.

1

Performance rating

What they delivered this year. You have this already — it comes out of the review cycle.

Already in hand
2

Pay position

Where their current base sits inside the range for their role. Ask HR for it before you allocate, not after.

The one to go find
If you fix one thing this year

Find the strong performer who’s paid low in range — and spend there first.
